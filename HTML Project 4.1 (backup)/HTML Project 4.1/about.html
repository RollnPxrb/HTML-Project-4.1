About Roll an Go Rentals

Driven by innovation, built by students.

Our Story

Roll an Go Rentals started out as a simple vision born inside the classrooms of the Technological Institute of the Philippines (TIP). As college students tackling complex challenges for our Information Technology project, we wanted to build something practical, efficient, and deeply connected to everyday mobility needs.

Recognizing the growing demand for reliable and accessible transportation options around the metro, our team brainstormed a streamlined car and vehicle rental platform. What began as a conceptual IT project transformed into a fully realized web application designed to give users a seamless booking and rental experience.
